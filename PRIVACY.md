## What the app does with your photos and videos
MyApp processes videos **only on your device**. Your photos, videos and their metadata are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We run no server and have no account system.

The app needs access to your photo library to:
- find large videos and show their size, date and thumbnail
- read the original video and save a smaller copy
- delete originals, but only after you confirm in the iOS system dialog

App settings (quality, minimum size, list of processed videos) are stored locally on your device in the app's preferences.

## Advertising (free version)
The free version shows ads from **Google AdMob** (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Before any ad, users in the EU/EEA/UK are asked for consent via Google's consent form. Depending on your choices, Google may process:
- device identifiers (IDFA only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt)
- IP address, coarse location, device and app information
- ad interaction data

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalized ads; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalized ads and fraud prevention. You can change your choice at any time in the app settings and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Your rights
You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information). Since we store no personal data ourselves, requests about ad data go to Google.
